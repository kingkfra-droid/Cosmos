🌌 Cosmos

Science Fact-Checking Platform

Cosmos is an open-source science fact-checking project dedicated to reviewing viral claims, debunking common myths, and separating scientific evidence from misinformation using reliable sources and peer-reviewed research.

Open-source project welcoming contributions from scientists and enthusiasts
